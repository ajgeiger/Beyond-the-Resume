# Portfolio website pages

Alex Geiger's portfolio site has these pages.

## Home

The home page introduces Alex Geiger, Lead Applied AI Scientist, and the live projects on the site. It points visitors to his resume, GitHub and LinkedIn.

## Projects

The projects page lists the live apps: the News Topic Explorer and the Natural Gas Price Forecast. Each project has a short case study, a working app and the code behind it.

## News Topic Explorer

The News Topic Explorer is an interactive walk through a large news corpus, from broad themes down to individual headlines. It is built on a topic model Alex wrote, using MiniLM embeddings, UMAP, HDBSCAN and c-TF-IDF keywords, with headlines collected by Headline Radar.

## Natural Gas Price Forecast

The Natural Gas Price Forecast is a production forecast of front-month natural gas futures, published each trading morning. A model tournament compares Chronos-2, gradient boosting and Greykite with a naive baseline over three horizons, and the event winners are stitched into one published forecast. The work uses public data only.

## Resume

The resume page shows Alex's full resume, including his role as Lead Applied AI Scientist at Signature Aviation, earlier roles, and his MS in Data Science from Syracuse University.

## Ask

The Ask page is a question box about Alex's projects and experience. Answers are written from his resume and project write ups. Visitors can ask about his work, the projects on the site, how he works, and what he would bring to a team.
